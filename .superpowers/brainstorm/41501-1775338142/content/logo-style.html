What style of medieval "C" are you drawn to?

This sets the foundation — pixel grid, ornament level, and character

A. Illuminated Manuscript

Ornate medieval letterform with decorative border, pixel-grid construction, neon flourishes. Like a page from a digital Book of Kells.

B. Runic / Angular

Sharp geometric C with angular cuts, pixel-filled interior, rune-like accent marks. More Viking/Germanic than classical medieval.

C. Gothic Blackletter

Heavy, chunky blackletter C built from thick pixel blocks. Two-tone shading (magenta + dim magenta) gives depth. Cathedral weight meets 8-bit.
