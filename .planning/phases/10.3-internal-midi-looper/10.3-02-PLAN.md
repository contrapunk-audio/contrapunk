</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 4: Centralize destination ownership</name>
  <action>Add a final dispatch ledger keyed by resolved destination identity, role/voice slot, channel, and pitch, with counted owners per `Live` and `Loop`. First owner sends the physical attack; later identical owners only increment. A physical NoteOff occurs only when the final owner releases. Apply the same rule to external MIDI and the built-in synth. Track CC64 owners per destination/channel/origin: physical pedal-down on first owner and pedal-up only after the final owner clears. Keep origin explicit in the ledger; never infer it from MIDI channel or lane tag.</action>
  <verify><automated>cargo test -p contrapunk-tauri commands::engine::tests::loop_ownership --lib</automated></verify>
