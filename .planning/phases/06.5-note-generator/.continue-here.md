<context>
The user wants to choose which voice position they play in a multi-voice harmony arrangement. E.g., with 4 IAC outputs:
- "I am voice 1" (top) → harmonies fill voices 2,3,4 below
- "I am voice 4" (bass) → harmonies fill voices 1,2,3 above
- "I am voice 2" → voice 1 above, voices 3,4 below

This requires rewriting harmonize() to chain in both directions from the user's position. The existing chaining model only goes one direction. New harmonize_single_directed(note, state_idx, above: bool) will handle direction-aware interval generation.
