<decisions_made>

- Native prototype stays isolated from the production entry.
- One page, fixed ratio, no tabs, no main-page scrolling.
- One Setup dialog; arrangement blocks are navigation and real mixer controls.
- Main-page controls are performance-critical only.
- Mixer levels affect real built-in synth role gains.
- `Your register` does not transpose physical input.
- Strict Canon locks per-voice Mode but permits group Hold.
- Free Imitation unlocks per-voice Mode and Hold.
- Hold is explicit: Cancel releases now; Near/Phrase/Forever may continue after source release.
- Hidden Setup controls must not consume live MIDI CC.
- Built-in presets are immutable and customized through Save As.
- Global arrangement presets must map to real engine fields, not speculative groups or controls.
- Do not tune guitar detection without a captured real failure and regression.
- Optimized Rust builds are mandatory for guitar timing/sustain checks.
- Never broadly stage the dirty tree; never stage `.pi-subagents/`.

</decisions_made>
