## Corpus repair and freeze

The original capture utility duplicated its first 2,048-sample block. All 1,380 source plucks contained that exact defect. `ml/training/export_samples.py` now applies one independently auditable policy:

1. Remove the duplicate only when the first two 2,048-sample blocks are byte-identical.
2. Resample actual audio to 48 kHz before writing the 48 kHz header.
3. Find three consecutive offline YIN frames within 0.75 semitone of the annotated string/fret pitch; use the end of the complete three-frame evidence run.
4. Rank eligible source plucks by expected-period autocorrelation minus spectral flatness, with source order as the only tie-breaker.
5. Retain 10 ms pre-roll and one 250 ms note, apply a 5 ms fade, pad to 500 ms, and gain-normalize to 0.8 peak without clipping.
6. Freeze reproducible onset provenance as the first 128-sample frame reaching 10% of that fixture's peak frame RMS.

Curation does not consult capture-time detector validation/confidence/peak/RMS, Contrapunk runtime output, filename exceptions, or holdout membership. Two isolated regenerations were byte-identical and matched the checked-in 138 by-class, 18 showcase, 10 confused-pair WAVs, onset TSV, and manifest.

A fresh anonymous 24-fixture waveform/CQT audit classified 24/24 as valid immediate clean isolated notes, with no delayed noise, multiple/changing events, or visible clipping. The audit was blind to filenames, labels, detector output, and prior results. This validates the uniform policy sample; it is not a claim of broad microphone, guitar, tuning, or polyphonic generalization.
