## Decisions Made

1. **Smart match priority** (06.4-02-01): Pattern matching now collects all possible matches across all candidate roots, then selects the best by: (1) most intervals matched, (2) root equals bass note, (3) earlier pattern position. This prevents ambiguous cases like A-C-E-F# being called "F#m7b5/A" instead of "Amin6".
