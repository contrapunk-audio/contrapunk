## Decisions Made
- Kept server/client modes in main.rs as they provide useful network MIDI functionality independent of the GUI
- Removed WASM-specific dependencies (wasm-bindgen, web-sys, js-sys, etc.) from root Cargo.toml since the contrapunk-wasm crate has its own dependencies
- Removed the entire GUI router (GUIRouterState, spawn_gui_router) from router.rs since the Tauri app has its own routing in src-tauri/src/commands/engine.rs
- Kept the CLI-style router (run_router) in router.rs for potential standalone use
